What is Eaglercraft?

Eaglercraft is Minecraft running directly in your browser. The game is compiled to WebAssembly, so you play with nothing to install and no heavy downloads: open the page, pick a version and you are in. It works on PC, phone and Chromebook with any modern browser, and you do not need a Minecraft account or a Mojang login to play.

Worlds are saved in the browser's own storage, so you pick up your game on the same device the next time you open the page. You can also play online through community servers: build with friends and join multiplayer sessions without touching your network settings. And yes, it is completely free, with no launcher to download and nothing else to set up.

1.12.2 or 1.8.8: which one should I pick?

Version 1.12.2 is the newest one. It runs on WebAssembly with garbage collection (WASM-GC), ships many more blocks and items, and gives you access to both your saved worlds and today's online servers. It is the best choice if your machine handles browser games well and you want the latest content.

1.8.8 is the classic: lightweight, stable and compatible with almost any browser, even on older or low-end computers. If you want maximum performance, traditional PVP or a machine with little memory, that is your version. Both open with a single click from the list above.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free to play Minecraft with Eaglercraft?

Yes, Eaglercraft is completely free. You can play Minecraft in the browser with no payment, no downloads and no time limits. Just pick a version and start building.

Do I need to buy Minecraft or create an account?

No. You do not need to own the game or create a Minecraft or Mojang account: the full game runs in your browser and starts straight at the main menu.

Can I play on my phone or a Chromebook?

Yes. Eaglercraft runs on phones, tablets, Chromebooks and PCs with a modern browser. Touchscreen devices show on-screen controls, and connecting a keyboard makes it even more comfortable.

How do I play with my friends?

Join the same server address as your friends from your browser. Each version includes a community server list, and you can also host your own server to play privately.

Are my worlds and saves kept?

Yes. Worlds are stored in the browser's local storage, so they are still there when you close the tab. Before clearing site data or switching devices, export a copy from the in-game menu.

Which version runs best on my computer?

If your machine is recent, choose 1.12.2: it uses WASM-GC and offers more blocks and servers. If your computer is slow or old, 1.8.8 is lighter and runs on almost any browser.
