Is Eaglercraft 1.21 out? Not yet

Short answer: no. As of 2026 there is no official Eaglercraft 1.21. The official project only publishes three playable versions: classic 1.5.2, 1.8.8 (the most used) and 1.12.2, which is the newest official build. If you find a site offering a «real Eaglercraft 1.21 download», it is almost always an older version with a new label.

Official development focuses on improving what already exists: the latest updates are for EaglercraftX 1.8 (u53) and PeytonPlayz585's 1.12.2 build, which is still in development. There is no 1.21 or 1.20 on the official servers.

What about Eaglercraft 1.20? Also missing

There is no official Eaglercraft 1.20 either, for the same reasons. Since Minecraft's 1.13 update («The Flattening»), Mojang rewrote large parts of the game: block and item IDs changed, rendering was modernized and content became data-driven. All of that makes the game heavier and much harder to fit into a browser tab.

On top of that, modern versions need more memory and CPU than Chromebooks and phones — exactly the devices most Eaglercraft players use — can comfortably provide. That is why official versions stop at 1.12.2 for now.

What is the newest Eaglercraft version?

1.12.2 is the most modern official build: it runs on WebAssembly with garbage collection (WASM-GC), includes the World of Color Update, new biomes, terracotta and concrete blocks, and connects to today's servers. 1.8.8 is the most popular: most public servers run on it and it is the best choice for PVP. Both are free and run straight in the browser.

So what is the «Eaglercraft 1.21» people talk about?

When a site promises an Eaglercraft 1.21, it is almost always referring to TuffClient or other community projects. These are fan-made modifications on top of the existing bases (1.8 and 1.12.2) with a modern visual style inspired by 1.21. They are not official versions and do not include real 1.21 content (trial chambers, the mace, the crafter, and so on).

They are fine to try as an alternative, but it helps to know what they are: community forks, not «Minecraft 1.21 in your browser». Here we keep the information focused on the official versions, which are the ones we link above.

Will Eaglercraft 1.21 ever be released?

It is possible, but not guaranteed. The project team and the community keep improving performance and server support, and browser technology (WebAssembly, WASM-GC, memory) keeps advancing. Even so, porting a modern Minecraft version to the browser remains extremely difficult.

If an official new version ever ships, we will update this very page and add it to the selector next to 1.12.2 and 1.8.8.
