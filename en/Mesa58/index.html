A bit of Venezuela..

Gafo/a

A synonym for stupid or clueless. A way of negatively judging someone's mental ability.

Nagüevona

An expression used mostly for complaints or exclamations, depending on the occasion.

Conchale

An expression of surprise, frustration or disappointment. The equivalent of the Chilean "darn" or "what a drag" when something goes wrong or you get a surprise.

Hablador de Paja

A person who goes around making false statements (lies).

Verciale

An expression of amazement that comes from "verga". Like the Chilean "no way" or "oh my god" when something surprises you.

Nagüará

An expression with different meanings depending on its context, such as "surprise", "complaint" or "disappointment".
