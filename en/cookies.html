4. How to give, refuse, or revoke consent

  • On arrival: the banner offers Accept all, Reject non-essential, and Customize (granular choice per category). Refusing does not prevent browsing.
  • At any time: click Manage cookies in the footer to reopen the banner and change your preferences.
  • From your browser: you can also clear site data in your browser's privacy settings (this erases your choice and we will ask again).

5. What happens if you decline

  • If you decline Statistics: the Cloudflare beacon is not loaded and we do not measure your visit.
  • If you decline Advertising: Google AdSense is not loaded in your browser and you will not see personalized ads from this site.
  • Essential features (language, session, apps) keep working.
